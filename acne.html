What Is Acne?

Acne is one of the most common skin conditions worldwide. It develops when pores become clogged with oil, dead skin cells and bacteria.

Common Symptoms

  • Whiteheads
  • Blackheads
  • Pimples
  • Cysts

Possible Causes

  • Hormonal changes
  • Excess oil production
  • Genetics
  • Inflammation

General Care

Gentle cleansing, moisturization and consistent sunscreen use may help support overall skin health.
