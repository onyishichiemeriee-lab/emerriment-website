9. Your Choices

Depending on the features available to you, you may be able to access, update, or delete certain information associated with your account.

You may also contact EMerriment regarding questions about your personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

EMerriment is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent or other legal requirements apply.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as EMerriment develops or as legal and operational requirements change.

Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how EMerriment handles information, please contact us through the official contact channels provided on the website.
